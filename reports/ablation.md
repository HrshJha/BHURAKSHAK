# Ablation status

Prior ablation metrics used the leaky v1 feature store. Their artifacts are preserved only under `reports/superseded_leaky/` and are not current results.

The v2 corpus has no co-temporal neighbors, so Group C is gated and the spatial ablation arm is unavailable. Leakage-free grouped development ablations will be regenerated after Phase 5 tuning and reported in Phase 9.
